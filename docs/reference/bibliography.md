# Bibliography

This bibliography uses Nature-style numbered references. Research entries are
core citations for the manual; software and documentation entries identify
official references for implementation dependencies and integration behavior.

## Research References

1. Kaplan, E. L. & Meier, P. Nonparametric estimation from incomplete
   observations. *J. Am. Stat. Assoc.* **53**, 457-481 (1958).
   doi:10.1080/01621459.1958.10501452.

2. Ebbinghaus, H. *Ueber das Gedaechtnis. Untersuchungen zur experimentellen
   Psychologie*. Duncker & Humblot, Leipzig (1885). English translation:
   *Memory: A Contribution to Experimental Psychology*. Teachers College,
   Columbia University (1913).

3. Cormack, G. V., Clarke, C. L. A. & Buettcher, S. Reciprocal rank fusion
   outperforms Condorcet and individual rank learning methods. In *Proc. SIGIR

4. Bruch, S., Gai, S. & Ingber, A. An analysis of fusion functions for hybrid
   retrieval. *ACM Trans. Inf. Syst.* **42**, 1-35 (2024).
   doi:10.1145/3596512.

5. Robertson, S. & Zaragoza, H. The probabilistic relevance framework: BM25 and
   beyond. *Found. Trends Inf. Retr.* **3**, 333-389 (2009).
   doi:10.1561/1500000019.

6. Carbonell, J. & Goldstein, J. The use of MMR, diversity-based reranking for
   reordering documents and producing summaries. In *Proc. SIGIR 1998*,
   335-336 (1998). doi:10.1145/290941.291025.

7. Malkov, Y. A. & Yashunin, D. A. Efficient and robust approximate nearest
   neighbor search using Hierarchical Navigable Small World graphs. *IEEE
   Trans. Pattern Anal. Mach. Intell.* **42**, 824-836 (2020).
   doi:10.1109/TPAMI.2018.2889473.

8. Campello, R. J. G. B., Moulavi, D. & Sander, J. Density-based clustering

9. Vitter, J. S. Random sampling with a reservoir. *ACM Trans. Math. Softw.*
   **11**, 37-57 (1985). doi:10.1145/3147.3165.

10. Hu, Y., Koren, Y. & Volinsky, C. Collaborative filtering for implicit

    doi:10.1109/ICDM.2008.22.

11. Abbas, A. K. M., Tirumala, K., Simig, D., Ganguli, S. & Morcos, A. S.
